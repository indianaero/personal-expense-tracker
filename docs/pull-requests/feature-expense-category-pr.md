## How to test
1. Sign in and navigate to `/categories`
2. Confirm global default categories are listed and cannot be edited or archived
3. Click the FAB to create a new category; confirm it appears in the Active tab
4. Click a custom category card to open the edit modal; rename it and save
5. Archive a custom category from the edit modal; confirm it moves to the Archived tab immediately (optimistic update)
6. Switch to the Archived tab and restore the category; confirm it returns to Active
7. Confirm the category appears in the expense form category selector
